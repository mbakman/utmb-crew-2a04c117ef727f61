For Les Contamines go down-valley via Saint-Gervais — never via Chamonix

The Chamonix > Contamines shuttle’s last outbound is 20:30 Friday — finished before Suha’s arrival window at km 32.5 even opens, and it runs one way only. Saint Gervais <> NDG runs every 20 min until 01:00 — straight through that window, past the 00:00 cutoff, and on your side of the valley. Take the train down; Chamonix never enters the plan.

How to read this sheet And what the poster does not tell you
“every N min → HH:MM” — meaning UNVERIFIEDThe source does not state whether that closing figure is the last departure or the end of service, and nobody has confirmed which. Treat it as the last moment you can rely on, be at the stop 20 min early, and never let the final connection of the night depend on it.
‡ The 23:59 on line 4The poster prints “23:59M”. That is a typographic error in the official artwork — the other cells read “02:00AM”, “01:00AM”, so an “A” has been dropped. It is 23:59.
One way vs two wayReproduced exactly as the poster prints each line. Lines 1, 5 and 8 are one-way. For line 8 a return must physically exist — it runs to 03:00 Sunday — but none is published, so do not plan on it.
Times past midnightA bracketed date means the time belongs to the following day: line 2’s “02:00 (29/08)” is 2 a.m. on the Saturday.
Two Chamonix hubsGrépon Bus serves the race start; Maison Nordique serves runner-following — except the St Gervais direction. The Chamonix map legend qualifies item 9 in as many words: “Race tracking – except St Gervais direction UTMB”. That exception is line 1, and the timetable duly boards it at Grépon Bus. They are ~10 min apart on foot and the wrong one costs an hour.
Nothing returns to Les HouchesVerified against the official 20-line index: the only line touching Les Houches is Houches > Chamonix — one way, one run, 16:00 Friday.
On boardBoarding is by the QR code emailed at booking; all of one person’s journeys sit under a single code. No children under 3 — both posters. On animals and wheels the two disagree: the FR follower timetable, the primary source here, forbids pets, bikes and scooters outright (“il est interdit d’emmener des animaux de compagnie, vélos et trottinettes”); the EN Chamonix poster only recommends against them and leaves each driver free to refuse. Plan on the FR rule.
Timetables can change mid-eventBoth posters warn that times may vary — but they name different channels. The FR follower timetable (primary) says any change is communicated via the website (“via le site internet”); the EN Chamonix poster says by SMS. Watch both: carry the number on the booking, and check the UTMB site before you leave.

Passes & booking

Zone-based. Passes are sold per race and per country. Champex-Lac and La Fouly need the SWITZERLAND pass. Courmayeur and Arnouvaz need ITALY. Everything on the France band needs FRANCE. Check every crew member’s QR code covers the zone before leaving the flat — a wrong-zone pass is refused at the door of the bus.

Booking closed 12 July 2026. No on-site sales and resale is not permitted — nothing further can be bought now. Board with the QR code emailed at booking; all of one person’s journeys are grouped under a single code. Screenshot it offline.

Road closures — no private cars

Roads and parking are closed to private cars for the duration of the event at Les Contamines, Saint-Gervais, Courmayeur, La Fouly, Champex, Col de la Forclaz, Trient and Vallorcine. Access is only possible by UTMB Mobility shuttle — or, where it runs, by train.
